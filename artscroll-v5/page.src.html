ArtScroll Digital Library for iPad

A redesign where the seforim lead and the app stays out of the way. Each screen is drawn at true iPad size and scaled to fit. The frames are live: the sidebar, tabs, the Aa א menu and the line selection all respond.

Home, three layouts

Same colours and content, different structure. Pick the one that should carry through the app.

A. Reading now (recommended). The open volume's cover leads, with today's learning as a row of covers.

B. Hebrew-led. No large cover: the current daf, set in Hebrew, is the headline.

C. Daf-first. Home is mostly the open page itself; one tap continues.

The app, in layout A

Library, the reader in landscape, portrait and Night, Settings and Zmanim.

Library. Covers in a five-across grid, filtered by collection, with an offline notice written into the page.

Reader, landscape. The Vilna daf beside the translation; the line being translated is tinted in both. Tap any line of Gemara.

Reader, portrait. The translation as a half-height sheet, with the Aa א menu open. Try the theme swatches, the slider and the switches.

Reader, Night. The same page after dark, with the accent lifted so it stays readable.

Settings. Short grouped lists; everything about how the page looks now lives in the Aa א menu.

Zmanim. A location chooser in place of the old "LocationUnknown" screen. Pick a city to see the day's times.
