    Questions

    Before you start.

    Is Loupe really free?

    Yes. Loupe is free to use, not a trial that runs out. Paid plans add more for heavy users and for studios that want one shared library.

    Where can I save from?

    Any website through the Chrome extension, any app on your iPhone through the share sheet, and your camera roll through the Loupe iOS app. Everything lands in the same library.

    What does Loupe actually tag?

    Colors by name (rust, sage, warm white), materials and finishes (bouclé, fluted oak, unlacquered brass), style and era, mood, and the pieces in the room. You can add or remove your own tags at any time.

    How does sharing with my studio work?

    Make a board for a project and share it with your team. Everything anyone saves to it shows up for everyone, tagged and credited to whoever found it.

    Can I get my images out?

    Yes. Download any board as a zip, or drag images straight into Canva, Figma or any canvas in your browser from the Chrome sidebar.

    Does it work with Figma and SketchUp?

    Today you can drag from the Loupe sidebar in Chrome onto any browser canvas, Figma included. A dedicated Figma plugin and a SketchUp extension are on the way.
