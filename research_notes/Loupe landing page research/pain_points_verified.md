# Verified pain points: how interior designers and design studios save, find and share inspiration images (second pass, November 2026)

This pass re-checks the first-pass notes (`pain_points_voice_of_customer.md`) against the original pages and adds new first-person evidence.

**How it was done.** Pages were fetched with curl and converted to text. Quotes below are copied from that text. App Store reviews come from Apple's public customer-reviews RSS feed:
- Pinterest 429047995 (US, GB, CA and AU most recent, plus US most helpful)
- Houzz 399563465 and Houzz Pro 1488002482
- Morpholio Board 761867957
- Milanote 1433852790
- mymind 1520332347
- Cosmos 1577975475
- Are.na 1299153149
- Moodboard 1465390447
- HomeBoard 1612805374

The feed URL pattern is `https://itunes.apple.com/{cc}/rss/customerreviews/page={n}/id={id}/sortby=mostrecent/json`. The feed returns at most 500 reviews per sort and country, so Pinterest's US "most recent" feed covers only 27 Oct to 7 Nov 2026. I found no Programa iOS app.

**Blocked sources.** These were respected, not worked around:
- fastcompany.com (403)
- ft.com (refused by the search tool; URL unknown)
- Pinterest investor relations (403)
- Houzz discussions (bot "Client Challenge" page)
- Mumsnet, PetaPixel and TipRanks (403)
- YPulse (bot challenge)
- Reddit (not attempted, per instructions)
- A third-party copy of the Wired article, refused by the session's safety check. The Wired original was then read on wired.com.

**Labels.**
- Speaker: **ID** interior designer; **OD** other designer or artist; **SO** studio or firm owner/principal; **C** consumer; **J/V** journalist or vendor.
- Status: **Verified** (wording, speaker and source match); **Corrected** (the claim exists, but wording, speaker, date or source differ, with the correct version given); **Could not verify** (original unreachable, or the text is not on the page).

## 1. Do the Business of Home, Futurism, FT, Fast Company, Wired, 404 Media and Pinterest Community items say what the first pass claims?

### Takeaway
Most of the important claims are real, but many first-pass quotes had the wrong wording, the wrong speaker or the wrong source:
- Business of Home: the "dreads" quote is not Claudia Martin's, and the article dates from 2019.
- Futurism: it contains no garden-designer quote.
- Tom's Guide: the "chairs defying physics" line is actually from Wired.
- Pinterest Community: two of the three threads are not from designers or not from late 2025.

The design-firm Pinterest ban thread is verified, with even stronger wording than the first pass gave. The FT article and the body of the Fast Company "library of Alexandria" piece could not be checked against the originals.

## 2. What do interior designers say, first-person, about losing or not finding saved images, screenshots, Pinterest getting worse, and sharing references with clients and teams?

### Takeaway
- **Pinterest getting worse:** the evidence is now strong and current. October–November 2026 App Store reviews from people using Pinterest for interior design say it is "almost entirely AI content", and a recent update buried saved pins behind recommendations.
- **The professional voice** comes from four places:
  - a design-firm owner whose decade of references vanished in a ban
  - a UK designer duo whose clients now bring boards with "unknowingly pinned AI images"
  - studio owners describing hunting through their phones after trade shows
  - designers on Morpholio, mymind and Cosmos describing re-creating boards, dumping screenshots, and forgetting why they saved something
- **"I know I saved it somewhere" in a named interior designer's own words** remains scarce. The closest are the CLOTH & KIND co-founder (section 3) and App Store reviewers.

## 5. What is Loupe's core pain point for each audience, and when is it felt?

### Takeaway
- **Individual designer:** "I can't get back to the real reference I saved, at the moment I need it."
- **Studio owner:** "The studio's eye lives in people's phones and accounts, so it is not ours."

### Cited Findings

**Individual interior designer: core pain (evidence-backed definition).** A working designer's visual memory is scattered across places that cannot be searched the way designers remember images:
- personal Pinterest boards, Instagram saves and screenshots
- camera-roll photos from markets and showrooms
- Dropbox and InDesign folders, and WhatsApp threads

Designers think in material, colour, era and mood, and clients "can't speak our design language" ([BOH 2020](https://businessofhome.com/articles/how-useful-are-inspiration-images)). The default public library has turned against them on three counts:
- It is filling with AI rooms that clients "unknowingly" pin ([Livingetc, Oct 2026](https://www.livingetc.com/features/the-truth-about-ai-homes)) and with ads ([Wired, Dec 2025](https://www.wired.com/story/pinterst-ai-slop-content/)).
- Its Oct–Nov 2026 updates bury saved pins behind "more ideas" and recommendations (App Store reviews, section 2).
- It can delete a decade of saves without human review ([Pinterest Community, Nov 2025](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/my-pinterest-account-was-suspended-by-mistake-10-years-of-professional-work-lost/40372)).

The result is that the designer cannot reliably get back to the real reference they saved, together with its source and the reason they saved it. They either search "forever" or start again.

When the individual designer feels it:
1. Back from a market or showroom trip, scrolling the phone: "having to go through your phone later… Oh, gosh, what vendor was this" ([StyleRow/CLOTH & KIND](https://intercom.help/stylerow/en/articles/10035435-building-new-efficiencies-in-the-design-process)).
2. Building a moodboard or presentation and not trusting what is real: "triple check my sources" ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/pinterest-is-drowning-in-a-sea-of-ai-slop-and-auto-moderation/)); "real images of real homes" (App Store).
3. Reaching for a saved image and getting recommendations or endless scrolling instead: "Where are the rest of my 1,000 plus pins???" (App Store, Oct 2026); "can't find any of my 'saves' on Pinterest without searching forever" (mymind review, 2024).
4. Needing the source or credit for a client or a post: Melissa Lee ([BOH, Mar 2026](https://businessofhome.com/articles/cosmos-wants-to-be-more-than-just-pinterest-for-the-cool-kids)); "why I saved something" (Cosmos and mymind reviews).
5. A client arrives with a board of AI-generated or homogeneous images ([Livingetc](https://www.livingetc.com/features/the-truth-about-ai-homes); [BOH 2023](https://businessofhome.com/articles/6-designers-on-how-they-make-the-most-of-inspiration-images)).
6. Opening the app to find the account suspended ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/14/pinterest-finally-admits-mass-bans-were-a-mistake-caused-by-an-internal-error/); [Pinterest Community](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/my-pinterest-account-was-suspended-by-mistake-10-years-of-professional-work-lost/40372)).

**Studio owner: core pain (evidence-backed definition).** In a typical interior studio of one to three people ([Pearl 2024](https://pearlprograms.s3.amazonaws.com/2024-survey/2024_INTERIOR_DESIGN_BUSINESS_SURVEY_FINDINGS_INSIGHT_REPORT.pdf)), the firm's accumulated eye sits in individuals' phones, personal Pinterest accounts and heads, not in something the business owns:
- the vetted references, past selections and "everything you've ever loved"

So it is fragile in two ways:
- One platform ban can erase "a decade of research, design selections, and creative references… essential to my projects and collaboration with my team" ([Pinterest Community](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/my-pinterest-account-was-suspended-by-mistake-10-years-of-professional-work-lost/40372)).
- One departure can make "the rest of the team kind of fell apart" ([BOH, Zajac](https://businessofhome.com/articles/how-this-indiana-designer-built-a-process-for-making-great-hires)). Low's diagnosis is that a firm which flounders when someone leaves is a "brokerage" ([BOH, Low](https://businessofhome.com/articles/business-advice-transition-after-employee-gives-notice)).

What principals say they want is a "single source of digital truth for design" shared across offices, so designers "don't have to start over" ([StyleRow/CLOTH & KIND](https://intercom.help/stylerow/en/articles/10035435-building-new-efficiencies-in-the-design-process)). They also want new hires brought into the studio's eye quickly ([BOH, Cox](https://businessofhome.com/boh/article/the-mentor)).

When the studio owner feels it:
1. An employee gives notice or leaves suddenly ([BOH, Low](https://businessofhome.com/articles/business-advice-transition-after-employee-gives-notice); [DesignDash](https://designdash.com/design/design-firm-friday-what-to-do-if-you-lose-a-key-team-member-suddenly/); [Galt](https://melissagalt.com/?p=55754)).
2. A platform account holding firm references is suspended ([Pinterest Community](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/my-pinterest-account-was-suspended-by-mistake-10-years-of-professional-work-lost/40372)).
3. Opening a second office or hiring the next designer, when sourcing must not "start over" ([StyleRow/CLOTH & KIND](https://intercom.help/stylerow/en/articles/10035435-building-new-efficiencies-in-the-design-process)).
4. A new hire's first two weeks ([BOH, Cox](https://businessofhome.com/boh/article/the-mentor)).
5. After a trade show, when everyone's photos sit on personal phones ([StyleRow/CLOTH & KIND](https://intercom.help/stylerow/en/articles/10035435-building-new-efficiencies-in-the-design-process)).
6. Preparing what to show a client, and when ([StyleRow/CLOTH & KIND](https://intercom.help/stylerow/en/articles/10035435-building-new-efficiencies-in-the-design-process); [Morpholio reviews](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=761867957/sortby=mostrecent/json)).

### Inferences
- **One-line hooks.** These are inferences built from the verbatim evidence above:
  - Designer: "You saved it. Now find it, by describing it, with its source."
  - Owner: "Your studio's eye, owned by your studio."
- The studio-owner definition rests on principals talking about people leaving and on one firm's lost account. The specific claim that image references leave with staff is an inference and should be validated in interviews.
- Because most studios are one to three people, the owner pain is best presented as the designer pain plus ownership, continuity and sharing, not as a separate enterprise problem.

### Gaps
- No verbatim quote from a named interior designer was found for the exact "I know I saved it somewhere" moment. The closest are the CLOTH & KIND phone-scrolling quote and App Store reviewers. Primary interviews with Loupe's users remain the best source.
